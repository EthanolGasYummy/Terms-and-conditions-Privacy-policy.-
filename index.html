Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026  ·  MCDuelsChecker#5569

01

Overview

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data MCDuels Checker collects (very little), how it's used, and your rights. We've kept this simple because the Bot is simple.

Short version: We don't store your personal data. The Bot fetches public Minecraft stats and shows them to you. That's it.

02

Data We Do NOT Collect

MCDuels Checker does not collect, store, or log:

  • Your Discord username, ID, or account details
  • The contents of your messages
  • Which servers you are in
  • Any personal information about you
  • Command usage history or analytics
03

Data Processed During Use

When you run a command like /rank <ign>, the Bot temporarily processes:

  • The Minecraft username or UUID you typed
  • Your Discord interaction ID (used only to send you a reply)

This data is not saved to any database. It exists only in memory for the duration of processing your request (typically under 2 seconds).

04

Third-Party Services

The Bot makes requests to the following third-party services to function:

  • MCDuels API (mcduels.com) — to fetch player stats and rankings
  • Discord API (discord.com) — to receive commands and send responses

These services have their own privacy policies. We have no control over how they handle data. The Minecraft usernames you look up are sent to the MCDuels API as part of the request.

05

Public Minecraft Data

All player statistics displayed by the Bot are already publicly accessible through the MCDuels website. The Bot does not expose any private information — it only reads and displays data that MCDuels has made available to the public.

06

Children's Privacy

The Bot does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no personal data at all, there is no special risk to minors from using this Bot beyond Discord's own policies.
